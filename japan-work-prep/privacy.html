Privacy Policy — Japan Work Prep

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Japan Work Prep (the "App") does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Your answers, scores, mistakes and language setting are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App. The App has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

The only network communication is with the store you installed the App from — Apple's App Store on iPhone, or Google Play on Android — when you subscribe or restore a purchase. Apple or Google processes that payment under its own privacy policy (Apple, Google); we never receive your payment details.

The payslip section is a built-in dictionary of payslip words. It does not take photos and does not send anything.

Listening questions are read aloud by your device's built-in Japanese voice. No audio is recorded.
