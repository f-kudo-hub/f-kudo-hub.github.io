Japanese test practice and payslip words for people working and studying in Japan: JLPT N5, JFT-Basic (A2), and the words on a Japanese payslip explained in your language.

Premium

Premium is an auto-renewable subscription: monthly or yearly. It unlocks every JLPT N5 and JFT-Basic question and mock test, the kanji, grammar and word lists, and all 35 payslip words. Without Premium you can try 5 questions of each test and 1 payslip word. There is no free trial.

To cancel: on iPhone, Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android, Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

New phone? Open the App and tap "Restore purchases" on the first screen.
