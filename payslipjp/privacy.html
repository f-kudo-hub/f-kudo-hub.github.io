Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Japan Pay Reader (the "App") explains a Japanese payslip in your language. This page explains what happens to your data.

The photo of your payslip

When you take or choose a photo, the App asks for your permission before sending anything. If you agree, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC, to read the payslip and write the explanation. The photo is not saved on our server and is not written to our logs. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models (Anthropic privacy policy).

A payslip can contain your name, employee number and company. You can cover them before taking the photo; the App does not need them. The AI is told not to repeat personal details in its answer.

What stays on your phone

The results (amounts and explanations), your language setting and your consent are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App. We do not keep a copy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). We never receive your payment details.

The App has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
